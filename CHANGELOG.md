# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-07)

First release.

* Runtime expansion of Cyrillic World Info keys (`key` and `keysecondary`) into regex keys with every
  word form and Unicode word boundaries.
* Dictionary morphology with Az.js 0.2.3 and the OpenCorpora dictionaries, fully client side.
* Paradigm union for invented names.
* Phrase agreement: adjective and noun, name chains, genitive dependents, hyphenated names.
* Homonym guard for proper names.
* Manual hints: gender, indeclinable, extra and excluded forms.
* In-memory and IndexedDB cache.
* Compile and Restore for lorebooks.
* Settings panel with preview and phrase tester, `/rumorph` slash command, Russian translation.
